# 0068: The pages fly

Status: landed (2026-10-19, 7426c0b)

Goal: the page's camera is the fly camera (item 0067): the mouse turns the view as it moves over the canvas with no button held, a finger turns it while down, WASD and the arrow keys walk, Space, E, Shift, Q and Control rise and sink, the gamepad's left stick walks and its right stick turns. The wheel, the pinch, the drag and the pan are gone (user, 2026-10-19). The scene's orbit, after the fit, is only the start.

Verify:
- `./build.sh test` passes, `./build.sh check` passes, `./build.sh web light` and `web models` build.
- The installed pages turn with the mouse, walk with the keys, and nothing happens on the wheel.

## Specification

- `src/engine/web_scene_page.odin`: `Scene_Page.orbit` becomes `fly: Fly`; `scene_page_init` sets `page.fly = fly_from_orbit(orbit_fit_width(...))` with the same fit call; `scene_page_camera` returns `fly_camera(page.fly, SCENE_PAGE_VERTICAL_FOV, 0.1, 100)`; `scene_page_label_screen` is unchanged. `SCENE_PAGE_WALK_SPEED` becomes `SCENE_PAGE_FLY_SPEED :: f32(5)`, metres per second, its comment likewise; `scene_page_walk` calls `fly_walk(page.fly, input, SCENE_PAGE_FLY_SPEED * delta_time)`, its comment dropping the orbit. `scene_page_gamepad`: the left stick and the d-pad through `fly_walk` at the same speed, the right stick through `fly_turn(page.fly, right * SCENE_PAGE_STICK_TURN_RADIANS_PER_SECOND * delta_time, 1)`, the trigger zoom removed with `SCENE_PAGE_TRIGGER_ZOOM_PER_SECOND`, the comment updated. Pointers: `on_scene_page_pointer_move` turns the view by the primary pointer's client delta (`event.data.mouse.pointer.is_primary`), `fly_turn(page.fly, movement, SCENE_PAGE_DRAG_RADIANS_PER_PIXEL)`, with the client delta kept as now and its comment; a mouse pointer (`event.data.mouse.pointer.pointer_type == .Mouse`) not yet in the list is added on its first move with no turn, a touch pointer is added on pointer down as now and removed on up and cancel as now; a new listener on the canvas for `.Pointer_Leave` removes the pointer, so the mouse coming back in does not jump. `scene_page_pointer_spread`, `scene_page_pointer_midpoint`, `scene_page_pan`, `on_scene_page_wheel` with its listener, `on_scene_page_context_menu` with its listener (the right button does nothing now, so the menu may open), `SCENE_PAGE_WHEEL_ZOOM_STEP` and `SCENE_PAGE_DISTANCE_MINIMUM` go; `SCENE_PAGE_DISTANCE_MAXIMUM` stays for the fit. The comments on `Scene_Page_Pointer` and `Scene_Page` name the new input.
- `src/demos/light/description.txt`, `cave`, `surface`, `models`: `Drag to orbit, pinch or wheel to zoom` (and the surface's `, WASD or the pad to move`) become `Move the mouse to look around, WASD to walk, Space and Shift to rise and sink. A finger looks on the phone, a gamepad walks and looks.`; the cave's `so zooming out shows the cave's inside` becomes `so walking out shows the cave's inside`.
- `doc/render.md`: in the camera paragraph the sentences from `Orbit input works through two pure steps` to `drifts nothing.` become: the page's input is the fly camera's: the mouse turns it as it moves over the canvas with no button held and a finger while down, through `fly_turn` at `SCENE_PAGE_DRAG_RADIANS_PER_PIXEL`, WASD and the arrow keys walk and E, Space, Q, Shift and Control rise and sink through `fly_walk` at `SCENE_PAGE_FLY_SPEED`, a gamepad in the standard mapping walks with the left stick and the d-pad and turns with the right stick, the sticks through `stick_dead_zone` so a resting stick drifts nothing, and there is no zoom and no pan; in the scene files paragraph `draws a scene on a canvas with the orbit input` becomes `draws a scene on a canvas with the fly camera's input`.
