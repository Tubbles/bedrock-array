# 0044: The art review sheet

Status: landed (2026-10-18, 9032a29)

Goal: step 5 of `doc/workflows/art_book.md`: one sheet per round that puts the blockout renders, the repaints, the turnaround and a squint row side by side, with the checks printed, so a round is judged in a minute. Refers to items 0042 and 0043.

Verify:
- `tools/art/sheet.py smelter --round 1` writes `work/art/smelter/round-1/review.png` and prints one line per check; a round without a turnaround still gets its sheet.

## Specification (main agent, 2026-10-18)

Binding facts: the blockout renders and masks under `work/art/<id>/blockout/` (`hero.png`, `front.png`, `left.png`, `back.png`, `right.png`, each with a `<view>_mask.png`, white on black, machine only, 1024 square); the round's images under `work/art/<id>/round-<n>/` (`<model>_<index>.<format>`) and its turnaround under `round-<n>/turnaround/<view>.<format>`; `data/art/families.sjson` and the brief's `family` key; Pillow is in the system Python (`generate.py` uses it), numpy is not.

- `data/art/families.sjson`: each family gets `palette`, a list of sRGB colours in 0..1, the furnace's: paint `[0.2, 0.24, 0.3]`, copper `[0.95, 0.64, 0.54]`, bare metal `[0.56, 0.57, 0.58]`, glow `[1.0, 0.55, 0.2]`.
- `tools/art/sheet.py`, system Python, Pillow only, `tools/materials` on `sys.path` for `sjson`. Command `sheet.py <id> --round <n>`. Rows of 512 pixel cells with a label under each (256 was too small, user), left to right: row 1 the blockout hero and the four views; row 2 the round's repaints sorted by name; row 3 the turnaround views that exist; row 4 every image of rows 2 and 3 as a 64 pixel greyscale thumbnail scaled up to the cell with bilinear sampling (the squint row: a blur, not blocks, user). A horizontal line at the hero mask's top row across rows 1 and 2 (the height line). Output `work/art/<id>/round-<n>/review.png`.
- The checks, one printed line each, `<CHECK> <image> <value> ok|warn`: `SILHOUETTE`, the edge coverage of the blockout mask's outline: the outline is the mask minus its 3 by 3 erosion, the edges are the greyscale repaint's FIND_EDGES above 32 of 255 dilated by 7 pixels (measured: 16 let a hero mask shifted 150 pixels pass on the grainy GPT repaints at 0.90, at 32 the right masks score at least 0.886 and the shifted ones at most 0.686; the blockout's own render scores 0.62 at 32, its base edge in shadow, and is not a repaint), the value the share of outline pixels that meet an edge; ok above 0.85. Hero repaints against `hero_mask.png`, turnaround views against their view's mask. `PALETTE`, the share of a repaint's pixels inside its mask within 0.25 (Euclidean, sRGB 0..1) of a palette colour; ok above 0.6. `HEIGHT`, the signed offset of the first row holding at least 4 edge pixels, inside the grown box's rows and the columns where the mask is set within its top 5 percent (the stack, which keeps the figure and the sky's grain out; a lone edge pixel anywhere in the box read the sky's grain, reviewer), from the mask's top row, over the mask's height; ok within 0.05. The first version read a foreground by colour distance from the image's border and scored the blockout's own render at 0.62, since the renders keep a horizon and cast shadows (implementer, 2026-10-18).
- Short procedures: the reading of the round's files, the edge map, each check as a pure procedure returning its value, the cell placement, `main` with argparse. Starting values, tuned on the first round's sheet.
- `doc/workflows/art_book.md` step 5 rewritten to the command, the output path and the three checks as implemented, no longer than it is.
