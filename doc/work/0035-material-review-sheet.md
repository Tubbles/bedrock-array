# 0035: The material review sheet

Status: designed (2026-10-18)

Goal: step 3 of `doc/workflows/materials.md`: `tools/materials/review.py` renders a baked set on a bevelled box with the wear rule, lays the maps out tiled so seams and repetition show, and prints the mechanical checks, so a set is judged before it lands and after every rebake. Refers to items 0028 and 0030.

Verify:
- `flatpak run org.blender.Blender -b --python "$PWD/tools/materials/review.py" -- painted_steel` writes `work/materials/painted_steel_review.png` and prints every check line ending in `ok`; the image shows the box with chipped edges, the user's judgement of the look.

## Specification (main agent, 2026-10-18)

Binding facts: the bake script of 0030 (reuse its SJSON reading and its OpenImageIO writing, by importing from `bake` what is general or by a small shared `tools/materials/images.py` if the implementer prefers, no duplication); the design probe of 0028 rendered a bevelled cube headless in Cycles on the CPU at 512 square in 1.8 s with 32 samples and denoising, a sun at rotation `(0.8, 0.2, 0.6)`, the camera at `(4, -4, 3.2)` with rotation `(1.1, 0, 0.785)`, a world background colour; Blender's Image Texture node has `projection = 'BOX'` with `projection_blend`, the Normal Map node a tangent space, the Map Range node `interpolation_type = 'SMOOTHSTEP'`. The wear rule and its parameters: `doc/workflows/materials.md`, The steps.

- `tools/materials/review.py`, run as `flatpak run org.blender.Blender -b --python "$PWD/tools/materials/review.py" -- <name>`. Inputs: the three maps `data/materials/textures/<name>_*.png` read with OpenImageIO into numpy floats 0 to 1, top row first; the preset `<name>` of `data/materials/library.sjson` (`albedo`, `wear`) and `tile_metres` of `data/materials/bake/<name>.sjson`, both through `sjson.parse`.
- The render, 1024 by 768, Cycles on the CPU, 32 samples, denoised: two cubes of edge 2 m with a bevel modifier (width 0.02, 3 segments), one at the origin and one at `(5, 7, 0)` so the far one shows the far scale; the sun (strength 3) and the camera of the probe; the world a flat `(0.3, 0.35, 0.45)`. The material: the three maps as Image Texture nodes (the albedo in the `sRGB` colour space, the other two `Non-Color`), `BOX` projection with blend 0.2, from the Object coordinate scaled by `1 / tile_metres` for the near scale and, for the far scale, the Object coordinate swizzled `zxy` (Separate XYZ and Combine XYZ) scaled by `1 / (tile_metres * 2.71828)`, near and far mixed by 0.5 for the albedo and the surface map; the normal map through a Normal Map node in tangent space at the near scale only (an approximation of the game's triplanar normal, say so in a comment); the wear rule in nodes with `d = 1 - abs(object coordinate)` per axis (the cube's half extent is 1), `edge = max(min(d.x, d.y), min(d.x, d.z), min(d.y, d.z))`, `wear = (field + amount * (1 - smoothstep(0, edge_width, edge))) > chip_threshold` (Map Range with `SMOOTHSTEP` for the smoothstep, a Math `GREATER_THAN` for the step); the Principled BSDF with base colour `mix(preset albedo * albedo map, wear.metal, wear)`, roughness `mix(surface.r, surface.g, wear)`, metallic `wear`.
- The sheet, 1024 by 1024, in numpy: the render on top, under it a 256 tall row of four 256 square panels: the albedo, the normal and the surface map each tiled 2 by 2 at 128 a tile, then the albedo tiled 8 by 8 at 32 a tile (box downsampled). Written with OpenImageIO, 8 bit RGB, to `work/materials/<name>_review.png` (the directory created if missing; `work/` is untracked).
- The checks, one line each, ending in `ok` or `check`: `SEAM <map> left_right=<> top_bottom=<> neighbour=<>` as the bake prints them, ok when both seams are at most 1.5 times the neighbour step; `REPEAT albedo peak=<>`, the largest value of the normalised autocorrelation of the albedo's grey (by FFT) outside a 16 texel radius of zero, ok below 0.5; `RANGE albedo min=<> max=<>`, the tinted albedo (the preset's `albedo` times the map's linear values) encoded to sRGB bytes, ok within 30 to 240; `RANGE roughness min=<> max=<>` over the surface map's R and G, ok within 0.05 to 0.95; `WEAR coverage=<>`, the share of texels whose wear field exceeds `chip_threshold`, ok within 0.02 to 0.4.
- Short procedures: reading, the scene, the material nodes (one procedure per group: the coordinates, the maps, the wear rule, the shader), the render, the panels, the sheet, each check, `main`.

Verify: the command above; the printed lines; `work/materials/painted_steel_review.png` read with the Read tool.
