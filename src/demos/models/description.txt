The smelter's generated models in three clusters, where a generated model is judged. Back left, the base models in a row: full, then the 700000 face limit. Centre, Tripo's low poly models in a column per technique, the face limit (24000, then 12000) and the smart low poly mode (limit 20000, no limit, then limit 12000), each column by triangle count toward the front. Front right, Blender's decimations: a column per base model (full, then 700000), a row per target (24000, then 10700). The nearest plaque of a column names it. Every low poly model has five configurations, the plain decimation and its four hole closings (fill holes, voxel remesh, Poisson, alpha wrap), each decimated to the plain one's count: the C key, the pad's North button and right shoulder, the name button on the page and the arrow button right of it cycle them, the pad's left shoulder and the arrow button left of the name step back. Move the mouse to look around, WASD to walk, Space and Shift to rise and sink. On the phone a finger looks, two fingers spread and pinch to walk and drag to move sideways and up, a gamepad walks and looks.
