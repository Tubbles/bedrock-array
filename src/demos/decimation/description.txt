The full smelter model beside Blender's decimations of its Poisson closing, from 768000 triangles down to 12000, halving each step, orbited one furnace at a time, with the Poisson depth of the closing as the configuration. Drag to turn the furnace, the wheel or a pinch to zoom, WASD to orbit and zoom from the keys. The arrows or the d-pad left and right step to the next furnace and keep the view, C, the pad's North button, the shoulders or the buttons at the bottom left cycle the depth (9, 8, 7, 6). A gamepad's sticks orbit as well.
